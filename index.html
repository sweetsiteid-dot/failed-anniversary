A Letter For Him 💙

Haesa, I just wanna say sorry for everything that happened between us.

Maybe when you first met me, you got the better version of me, the one who cared more, tried more, and knew how to treat you right. But as time went by, I know I became more selfish, stubborn, and sometimes even harsh towards you.

I'm really sorry for every word, attitude, and treatment that hurt you, especially for the things that you felt were too much. I never meant to hurt you or make you feel like I didn't care.

But I understand that even if I didn't mean it, it still hurt you, and for that, I'm genuinely sorry.

Thank you for being with me for these almost two years, Sa.

Thank you for staying through so many things, especially during the time when we were both chasing our dreams of getting into PTN.

We studied together, got stressed together, stayed busy together, and somehow, we got through it all together.

I'm really happy that I got to experience that whole chapter of my life with you.

Getting accepted together, celebrating together, and having someone beside me through all those difficult days is something I'll always be grateful for.

And I'm sorry if after we started college, I changed so much and ended up treating you differently.

Maybe I became too caught up with my own things and forgot that you were also someone who needed my time, attention, and understanding.

I'm truly sorry, Sa. I know I wasn't perfect, and I know there were many times when you deserved better from me.

I'm not saying this to make excuses for anything. I just want you to know that I realize my mistakes and I genuinely regret hurting you.

We didn't get to make it to our second anniversary on August 4, but I still don't wanna let those two years pass without saying thank you.

Thank you for loving me, for being there, for growing with me, and for being part of so many important moments in my life.

Whatever happens after this, I'll always be grateful that I got to have you in my life and experience all of those things with you.

I'm sorry for everything, Sa. Genuinely, I am.

Thank you for being a part of my story. 💙
